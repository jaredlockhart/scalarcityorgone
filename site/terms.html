About this site

scalarorgone.com is the website of Scalar Orgone, a small studio in Toronto that makes orgone pyramids, cloudbusters and accessories by hand. It shows the pieces currently for sale in our Etsy shop.

Buying

Nothing is sold on this site. Every piece links to its Etsy listing, and your order, payment, shipping and any returns are handled on Etsy under Etsy's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy and our Etsy shop policies.

Product information

Product names, prices and photos here are copied from our Etsy listings and updated regularly. If anything on this site differs from Etsy, the Etsy listing is correct. Prices are shown in Canadian dollars; Etsy may show them in your own currency.

Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or any tracking, and it doesn't ask for or collect personal information. As with any website, the services that deliver it see your IP address and browser details in order to send you the page: the site is hosted on Amazon Web Services and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. We don't receive or keep that information.

If you email us, we'll use your address only to reply to you.
